Overview

What is FunStack?

Most personal project pages are static resumes that only list finished work, or scattered links spread across GitHub, Scratch, and random hosting sites.

FunStack solves this by bringing everything into one place — a single, growing hub for websites, browser games, and Scratch creations, all built for fun and learning, and updated as new ideas come to life.
